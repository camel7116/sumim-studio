Notes
  - 389 x 800 px, PNG-32. Both the outside of the device AND the screen area are
    transparent; the Dynamic Island is opaque and belongs to the frame.
  - Measured screen rect (alpha flood-fill, sub-pixel, in the 389x800 image):
      x = 16.93, y = 13.73, width = 355.91, height = 772.94
    (right edge 372.84, bottom edge 786.68)
  - Measured screen corner: a continuous "squircle" corner, not a circular arc.
    Its diagonal matches a circular radius of 52.4 px; 53 px is the largest
    circular radius that still stays inside the real corner. We use 52 px.
  - Consumed by `src/components/ui/phone-frame.tsx`. The switch that turns this
    frame off again (back to the drawn CSS phones) is `deviceFrame` in
    `src/content/site.ts`.

    prohibited); it ships only as part of the site.
